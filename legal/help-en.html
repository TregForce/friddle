Contact

Questions, bugs or wishes? Write to us: friddle.app@outlook.com. In the app, the quickest way is the gear icon → “Send feedback”; the app version and device are then filled in for you.

Finger charts

Choose the key and the position at the top. The dots show where the fingers go on the strings. Arrows up or down mean the finger is placed a semitone higher or lower than in the basic hand frame. Switch the instrument (violin, viola, cello) in the gear menu.

Adding fingerings to sheet music

  • How it works: photograph a sheet or choose a PDF or photo. Friddle writes the finger above every note.
  • PDFs from notation software (e.g. MuseScore, Sibelius, Finale, Dorico) are read exactly. Scans and photos are read by image recognition – please check the result.
  • Better photos: good light, the whole page straight in the frame, no camera shake.
  • Corrections: tap a fingering to change or delete it; drag it to move it. Saved sheets stay in “Folder” and can be corrected later.
  • Higher positions: if a note lies outside 1st position, Friddle suggests a suitable position (Roman numeral). You can switch this off in the gear menu.
  • Sharing: “Share as PDF” sends the annotated sheet to e.g. GoodNotes, email or a printer.

Friddle Pro

The finger charts are free, and three sheets can be annotated for free. Friddle Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. On a new device or after reinstalling: gear icon → “Restore purchases” (with the same Apple ID or Google account).

Data

Sheet music, photos and corrections stay on your device. Friddle has no user account and no server of its own. Deleting the app also deletes its data. Delete single sheets in “Folder”. More in the privacy policy.
