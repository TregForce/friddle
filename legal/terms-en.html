Service

Friddle shows finger charts for violin, viola and cello and automatically adds fingerings to sheet music (1st position, optionally with suggestions for higher positions). The finger charts are free. Adding fingerings is free for a limited number of sheets; after that it can be used without limit with the one-time in-app purchase “Friddle Pro”.

Purchases

Friddle Pro is bought through Apple’s App Store or Google Play at the prices and terms shown there. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. Refunds follow Apple’s or Google’s rules. “Restore purchases” lets you use a purchase on other devices with the same account.

Automatic recognition

Fingerings are determined automatically; for scans and photos Friddle uses image recognition. Individual notes may be missing, extra or recognised wrongly. Please check the results. Friddle does not replace lessons.
