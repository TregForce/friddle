2. Services

Friddle is a practice and learning tool for string instruments. In particular it offers:

  • Finger charts for violin, viola and cello in all major and minor keys and several positions;
  • Adding fingerings: Friddle reads sheet music (PDF, scan or photo) and writes in fingerings – in 1st position, optionally with suggestions for higher positions;
  • Correcting, saving in the folder and sharing annotated sheets as PDF.

Friddle works without a user account. Sheet music is processed only on your device. The exact range of functions follows from the current version and the description in the App Store or on Google Play.
