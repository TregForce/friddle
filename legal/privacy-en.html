Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026 · Deutsch

In short

Friddle does not collect personal data. There is no user account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools and no server of our own. Your sheet music, photos and corrections stay on your device.

What is stored on your device

  • Sheet music and photos you open in Friddle are read on the device only. If you save a sheet to the folder, the file, your corrections and a small preview image are kept in the app’s storage on your device. Depending on your device settings they are part of your personal device backup (iCloud or Google). Friddle has no access to it.
  • Settings (e.g. key, position, language, dark mode) are stored on the device.
  • Unlock: whether Friddle Pro was purchased and how many free sheets were used is kept in the device’s protected storage.

Deleting Friddle deletes this data (on iPhone, the count of free sheets may remain in the keychain).

Camera and photos

Friddle asks for access to the camera or photos only when you want to photograph or choose sheet music. Images are processed on the device only and are not transmitted.

Purchases

Friddle Pro is bought through Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Payment and processing are handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies. Friddle receives no payment or account data; it only checks on the device whether the unlock was purchased.

Sharing of data

Friddle does not share data with third parties. When you share an annotated PDF (e.g. to GoodNotes, by email or to print), this happens through your device’s share sheet to the app you choose.

Feedback by email

“Send feedback” opens your own mail app with a prepared message. It contains the app version, operating system and device type, the chosen instrument and language, so that we can understand problems. You see the message before sending and can change or delete anything; it is only sent when you tap Send yourself. We use your email address and the content only to reply to you and to improve Friddle (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR) and delete the message once it has been dealt with, at the latest after two years. The same applies to photos or sheet music you attach yourself.

Web version

The web version of Friddle is served via GitHub Pages. When you load it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address (see GitHub’s privacy statement). In the web version, too, sheet music is processed and stored only in your browser.

Your rights

As Friddle does not collect personal data, we hold no data about you. Under the GDPR you nevertheless have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Questions are welcome at the email address above.
